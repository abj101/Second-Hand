Join equal numbers on a 4x4 grid. Arrow keys move every tile as far as it can go
in that direction. When two tiles with the same value collide they merge into one
tile with double the value. A new 2 (usually) or 4 (rarely) appears after each move.
The advertised goal is creating a 2048 tile. The game ends when no legal swipe remains.
You see only the screen; score is shown above the board.

Keep the highest tile in one corner (prefer bottom-left). Prefer Down, then Left,
so new tiles spawn on the opposite edge. Never repeat a swipe that just no-op'd.
A legal swipe always moves at least one tile.
