### W4.2
- `classify(history) -> str`: pure function over an entity's evidence pairs `(s1_px, s2_px, dcam_px, responded)`, so W4.3 can replay it from the trace. Rules exactly as world-memory.md §3.2: world `|d_w| < POS_TOL` with `|Δcam| ≥ 1 tile`; screen `s2 ≈ s1` with `|Δcam| ≥ 1 tile` and not self; actor `|d_w| ≥ 1 tile` not self; self = responds to directional input (`observer.changed` on a one-tile crop at the entity's position, before vs after a directional press, > 0.05), with `kind: player` as a prior; `unknown` until `ANCHOR_EVIDENCE = 2` agreeing pairs. Screen persistence across a scene cut in ≥ 2 places also gives `screen`; disappearing when a modal closes gives `transient: true`.
- `World` keeps `cam_px` (sum of `skill.scroll`; None scroll → no evidence that step) and per-entity evidence (last 6 pairs), checkpointed in `state()`.
- Actor-aware `fold` and staleness use `anchor == "actor"`; `ACTOR_KINDS` only for `unknown`.
- HUD regions: screen-anchored, non-transient entities get `region` = a one-tile box at `at`; each step `changed()` on that crop vs the last stored crop; the view's Screen line lists HUD names with their last-changed step.
- **Acceptance:** `pytest tests/agent/test_world.py -q`; full suite; golden episodes unchanged (no HUD/NPC in them) or re-generated only where anchors are new fields, with the diff reviewed.

### W4.3: `scripts/check_world.py anchors <run>`
- Replays `trace.jsonl` rows in order: entities + scroll + the before frames (`frames/NNNNNN.png`; the after frame of step N is the before frame of step N+1) through `classify`. Scores against the truth rule in §2. Prints a confusion matrix and the actor-wall count; exits 0 on the gate numbers.
