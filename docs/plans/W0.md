- **`memory.py`:** replace the lazy `from agent.space import exit_to` in `match_place` with a direct `tiles` lookup (same semantics).
- **Trace additions (contracts.md#trace, 2026-10-12):** `entities`: the before-perception entities as `[{"id", "name", "kind", "at"}]`; `scroll`: the skill's total `[dx, dy]` or null. Used by W4 offline.
